# 0.4.0-test4

A small update with three fixes:

- Restored lighting from placed and dropped lanterns.
- Fixed the Remake ammo HUD falling back to Classic while dual wielding.
- Fixed overlapping phone and objective text.

Download `cof-enhanced-0.4.0-test4.zip`, extract it into your Cry of Fear folder,
and run `Install.cmd`. To upgrade, use your existing Enhanced folder without
uninstalling first. Saves and settings are preserved.

The source ZIP is for developers. `SHA256SUMS.txt` contains archive checksums.
